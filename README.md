## 🍎 Add Your Virtual Card to Apple Pay

One of CoreGold's useful features is support for Apple Pay on eligible card products.

With a compatible card, you can use Apple Wallet to make payments at participating merchants.

### How to Add a Card to Apple Wallet

1. Open the **Wallet** app on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **+** button.
3. Select **Debit or Credit Card**.
4. Follow the instructions to add your eligible CoreGold card.
5. Complete any verification requested by the card issuer.
6. Once approved, use Apple Pay wherever your card and Apple Pay are accepted.

### Why Use Apple Pay?

- Convenient checkout without repeatedly typing card details
- Tokenized payment credentials at supported merchants
- In-app and online purchases
- Contactless payments where supported

**Important:** Not every CoreGold card is eligible for Apple Pay. Availability depends on the issuing bank, card product, device, and region.

## 📱 Add Your Virtual Card to Google Pay

Eligible CoreGold cards may also support Google Wallet.

### How to Add a Card to Google Wallet

1. Open **Google Wallet** on a compatible Android device.
2. Tap **Add to Wallet**.
3. Select **Payment Card**.
4. Enter your eligible virtual card details.
5. Accept the applicable terms.
6. Complete any required issuer verification.

After successful setup, you can use Google Pay at participating merchants.

For contactless in-store payments, your device must support NFC and meet Google Wallet requirements.

**Note:** Google Wallet compatibility varies by card issuer, country, and device.

## 🔐 Why Use Multiple Virtual Cards?

Managing multiple subscriptions with a single personal bank card can become complicated.

With multiple virtual cards, users can organize their expenses by category.

For example:

- **Card A:** AI subscriptions
- **Card B:** Cloud infrastructure
- **Card C:** Developer tools
- **Card D:** Digital advertising
- **Card E:** Other online services

This makes it easier to review transaction histories and separate spending.

Where supported, card controls can also help manage available balances and spending limits.

Remember that freezing or deleting a virtual card does not automatically cancel an existing subscription. Always cancel recurring payments through the merchant.

Transactions may still be subject to fraud monitoring, blockchain analysis, merchant verification, issuer controls, and applicable legal requirements.

Users should comply with relevant financial regulations and the terms of the services they purchase.
